    CaMML Spring School 2027 ↗

    Dates
    5–9 April 2027
    Location
    STFC Daresbury Laboratory, UK
    For
    PhD students in materials and molecular simulation who can code but are new to machine learning; a limited number of places may be available for industrial applicants
    Key dates
    Apply by 26 November 2026 · outcome by 17 December 2026 · payment by 13 February 2027
    Fee
    £250 per student, including 5 nights' accommodation, breakfast, lunches, coffee breaks, the school BBQ and poster session refreshments
    Places
    Limited to 40; if oversubscribed, the organisers will favour a diverse group of attendees
    Organised by
    PSDI and AIchemy, with support from STFC-SCD, CCP5, CCP9 and CECAM

    The Chemical and Materials Machine Learning School is an international training course on the latest machine learning methods for atomistic simulation of materials and molecules. Talks and hands-on sessions cover the basics of machine learning, machine learning interatomic potentials and graph neural networks, and attendees can present a poster on their work.
