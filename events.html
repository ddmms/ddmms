Events

Conferences, workshops, summer schools and seminars organised or hosted by the Data Driven Materials and Molecular Science group.

Workshops

  • 2026

    46th CCP5 Annual General Meeting and Conference ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · CCP5 Annual Meeting

    The 46th Annual General Meeting and Conference of CCP5 (University of Edinburgh, 9–11 September 2026), bringing together the community to showcase research and software developments in molecular and materials simulation.

  • 2026

    The Atomic Simulation Environment: Integration into Wider Community Projects ↗

    Co-Organiser: Alin Elena · CECAM Flagship Workshop

    A CECAM Flagship Workshop at CECAM-DE-SMSM (Mainz, Germany, 15–19 June 2026) bringing together developers of electronic structure codes and community packages built on ASE to test core redesigns, plugin architectures, and ensure compatibility for ASE v4.

  • 2026

    ESL Workshop 11: Connecting Library Developers and Technologies ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · CECAM / ESL Workshop

    The 11th Electronic Structure Library (ESL) workshop held in Zaragoza, Spain (9–11 February 2026), bringing together developers of shared computational libraries, electronic structure codes, and community organisations to coordinate library interfaces, integration technologies, and curated open-source tooling.

  • 2025

    Quantum Computing in Materials and Molecular Sciences ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · Royal Society Scientific Discussion Meeting

    A Royal Society Scientific Discussion Meeting at the Royal Society (London, UK, 6–7 October 2025) bringing together researchers and industry leaders in quantum computing, computational materials science, chemistry, and life sciences to evaluate current limitations and future opportunities.

  • 2025

    45th CCP5 Annual General Meeting and Conference ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · CCP5 Annual Meeting

    The 45th Annual General Meeting and Conference of CCP5 held at the University of Liverpool (8–10 September 2025), celebrating research and software advances in molecular modeling, sponsored by CCP5, the Henry Royce Institute, and the RSC Statistical Mechanics & Thermodynamics Group.

  • 2025

    The Atomic Simulation Environment Ecosystem: Present and Perspectives ↗

    Co-Organiser: Alin Elena · CECAM Flagship Workshop

    A CECAM Flagship Workshop at CECAM-HQ (EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland, 23–27 June 2025) bringing together core developers and users of the Atomic Simulation Environment (ASE) and its broader ecosystem to transition towards a distributed plugin architecture, community governance, and sustainable interoperability.

  • 2024

    44th CCP5 Annual General Meeting and Conference ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · CCP5 Annual Meeting

    The 44th Annual General Meeting and Conference of CCP5 held at the University of Sheffield (4–6 September 2024), sponsored by CCP5 and the RSC Statistical Mechanics & Thermodynamics Group, showcasing research from across the UK and international molecular simulation community.

  • 2024

    Electronic Structure Software Development: Advancing the Modular Paradigm ↗

    Co-organiser: Alin Elena · CECAM Flagship Workshop

    A CECAM Flagship Workshop and the 10th ESL workshop held at CECAM-HQ (EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland, 19–24 February 2024), focusing on modular electronic structure software development, shared community libraries (such as libxc, ELPA, Wannier90, spglib, and ELSI), robust interfaces, and sustainable software practices for modern HPC architectures.

  • 2023

    Open Science with the Atomic Simulation Environment ↗

    Co-Organiser: Alin Elena · CECAM Flagship Workshop

    A CECAM Flagship Workshop held at STFC Daresbury Laboratory (24–28 April 2023), supported by CECAM, Psi-k, CCP5, CCP9, the Ada Lovelace Centre, and PSDI, featuring research talks, hands-on tutorials for reproducible calculations with ASE, and developer code sprints.
